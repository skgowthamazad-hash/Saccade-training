How to Perform This Exercise

  1. Keep your head still: Sit comfortably at arm's length from your screen. Only move your eyes, not your neck.
  2. Anchor your gaze: Focus steadily on the central white dot.
  3. Quick saccade shift: As soon as a colored dot flashes in your peripheral vision, snap your eyes directly onto it.
  4. Return to center: Immediately shift your focus back to the center dot as the peripheral dot vanishes.
  5. Rest & Blink: Remember to blink naturally. Stop immediately if you feel eye strain or dizziness.
